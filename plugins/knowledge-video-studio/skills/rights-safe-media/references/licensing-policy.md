# Licensing and provenance policy

## Accepted automatic classes

- `Public Domain` or an equivalent machine-readable public-domain mark.
- `CC0`.
- `CC BY`, provided attribution is retained.
- `CC BY-SA`, provided attribution and share-alike review are retained.
- `Pexels License`, subject to current Pexels terms.
- `Pixabay Content License`, subject to current Pixabay terms.
- User-owned material with an explicit rights-basis note.

Licenses containing `NC`, `ND`, “all rights reserved,” unknown text, or missing metadata require manual review and are not automatically downloadable from Commons.

## Required ledger fields

`id`, `provider`, `provider_asset_id`, `creator`, `source_page`, `license`, `license_url`, `retrieved_at`, `file_path`, `sha256`, `segment_ids`, `attribution`, and `rights_notes`.

## Automatic blockers

- Missing or non-HTTPS source page for remote media.
- Missing license name or license URL.
- File missing or checksum mismatch.
- Commons license not in the accepted set.
- A direct media URL with no stable provider page.
- A clip ripped from a social platform, news broadcast, movie, show, or music video without separate permission.

## Manual-review flags

- Recognizable people in advertising, medical, financial, political, criminal, or other sensitive contexts.
- Prominent trademarks, copyrighted artwork, screens, packaging, or private property.
- Historical material whose date, location, or event identity is relied upon as fact.
- AI-generated elements that could be mistaken for documentary evidence.
- CC BY-SA material in a distribution context whose share-alike obligations are unclear.

Always recheck the provider's current official terms before final publication. This policy does not provide legal advice.
